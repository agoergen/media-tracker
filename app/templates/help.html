1. Core Philosophy

LeisureLedger is designed as a focused, distraction-free personal ledger for cinema, television, video games, literature, and the performing arts. Unlike algorithmic social networks with endless feeds, LeisureLedger centers on intentional media consumption, chronological logging, annual goal-setting, and rich historical metrics.

⏳ Ascending Chronology
Media lists sort in ascending order (oldest to newest) to preserve a true chronological timeline of your year.
📦 Collapsed by Default
Detailed summaries, posters, and technical metadata stay cleanly collapsed until you click to expand them.
🔍

2. Universal Search & The 3 Action Cards

You can search external databases directly from the Homepage Hero, any media listing page, the Up Next queue, or the Goals screen.

Integrated Metadata Sources:
  • Movies & Television: The Movie Database (TMDB) API
  • Video Games: Twitch Internet Game Database (IGDB) API
  • Books: Google Books API (with OpenLibrary fallback)
  • Stage Theater: Internet Broadway Database (IBDB) & Wikipedia/Wikidata

The 3 Action Cards Workflow

Whenever you search and select a title, LeisureLedger displays three side-by-side action cards. Depending on where you originated your search, the recommended action will automatically highlight:

1. Track Completed

Log Completed Entry

Immediately logs the title into your official ledger. Record completion date, revisit status, custom formats/platforms, rating, and notes.

Best for: Media you have just watched, played, read, or attended.
2. Queue Up Next

Add to Backlog Queue

Places the title on your active "Up Next" queue without logging completion. Holds your place until you finish it.

Best for: Short-term queue (max 10 titles) you plan to experience soon.
3. Set as Goal Target

Designate Target Title

Anchors the title as one of your 3 annual challenge targets for the category. Earns a ⭐ star upon completion.

Best for: Annual reading/gaming/viewing milestones and challenges.

3. Media Ledgers & Tracking Mechanics

LeisureLedger organizes your media into five specialized categories. Each category contains customized fields, statistics, and filters:

🎥 Movies

Tracks release year, directors, writers, leading cast, runtime, certification ratings, box office financials, and viewing platforms (e.g. Theater, 4K Blu-ray, Netflix, Apple TV).

📺 Television

Tracks TV series by individual seasons. Stores season episode counts, original network (e.g. HBO, FX, Netflix), release year, and personal scores.

🎮 Video Games

Tracks completion date, platform played (e.g. PC, Switch, PS5), franchise name, developers, publishers, genre tags, and completion variants (e.g. Main Story, 100% Completion, DLC Expansion).

📚 Books & Literature

Tracks authors, page counts, StoryGraph/Goodreads ratings, genre tags, and media format (Audiobook, Ebook, Physical, Graphic Novel).

🎭 Stage Theater

Tracks live theatrical plays and musicals. Stores original Broadway theater, location/venue attended (default: Orpheum), show type (Musical/Play), runtime, and synopsis.

Note on Theater Artwork: Because stage theater lacks a central artwork database, cover images are more limited and sourced via an optional follow-up Wikipedia image search (which may return varying results depending on the show). You can also upload your own custom poster or playbill image directly on the tracking confirmation form.
🔄 Revisit / Rewatch / Replay / Reread Tracking

Whenever you rewatch a movie/show, replay a game, or reread a book, check the "Revisit" box. Revisit tracking separates brand-new discoveries from beloved re-experiences, powering the dual-progress bar system and revisit rate metrics.

📋

4. The "Up Next" Backlog Queue

The Up Next queue (accessible via the navigation bar) is your active holding area for media you intend to consume in the near future.

🎯 Strict 10-Item Limit
Each category is capped at 10 active items. This prevents backlog inflation and forces conscious curation of what you actually plan to experience next.
⚡ Single-Click Tracking
When you finish an item on your queue, simply click the Track button on its card to instantly log it into your ledger with today's date.
⭐ Goal Correlation Badge
If a queued item is also one of your active annual goal targets, it is highlighted with a gold star marker (⭐ Target Title).
🎯

5. Annual Goals & The Star Reward System

Goals in LeisureLedger are bound to individual calendar years (e.g. {{ datetime.now().year }}). You can set both numerical volume goals and specific priority targets.

Understanding the Dual-Progress Bars

New Media Progress Bar (Dark / Green)
Counts only brand-new media entries (excluding revisits). This is your primary goal driver. When this bar fills, your annual goal is complete!
All Media Progress Bar (Red)
Counts total media items logged during the year including all revisits, rewatches, replays, and rereads.

Target Titles & Star Badges (⭐ ⭐ ⭐)

Assign up to 3 specific target titles per category for the year. Whenever you track a target title, you earn a star badge on your dashboard.

🔄 "Validate All Targets" Button
If you tracked a title before creating a goal target, or if a title didn't immediately mark complete, click "Validate All Targets" on the Goals page. The backend will retroactively cross-reference all logged titles for that year and award your earned stars.
📊

6. Metrics & Historical Benchmarks

The Metrics page provides deep analytical breakdowns of your media consumption habits across individual years or All Time:

📚 Book Formats & Authors
Visual distribution of Audiobook vs Physical vs Ebook reads, co-author counts, and total pages consumed.
🎮 Game Platforms & Franchises
Breakdowns of platforms played (PC, Switch, PlayStation, Xbox) and your top completed gaming franchises.
📺 TV Episodes & Revisit Rates
Exact total episode counts, season rewatches, and provider distributions.
📈 Historical Benchmarks
Compares your current year pace against your historical annual average and personal record peak year.
🔒

7. Privacy Controls & Public Profiles

LeisureLedger gives you full granular control over what is visible to the public:

🔐 Per-Item Privacy Toggle

When logging or editing any movie, show, game, book, or stage performance, you can check "Keep this entry private". Private items are completely hidden from public visitors and shareable profiles. However, your private ledger totals and goal calculations remain 100% accurate.

🌐 Shareable Public Profile URL

In your Account Settings (/account), you can toggle "Make my ledger profile publicly accessible". This creates a clean public URL (/<your-username>) that you can share with friends. Visitors can view your public ledger and annual statistics without needing an account.

📋 Public "Up Next" Sharing

You can optionally enable "Include 'Up Next' queue on my public profile" to let visitors see what movies, books, and games you are planning to experience next.
